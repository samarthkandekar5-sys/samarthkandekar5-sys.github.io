FOUNDER STORY
VelCura began with a family observation. Samarth saw his mother visit clinics for minor illnesses, with visits costing around ₹500–₹700; some of the illnesses passed within a couple of days. It prompted a question about how people can find clearer, appropriate guidance when they are unsure where to begin. He started exploring the problem in October 2025. The product idea is to make care journeys easier to navigate while keeping clinical decisions with qualified professionals.

WHAT WE ARE EXPLORING
VelCura Health: An early-stage healthcare technology venture exploring a patient platform and clinic intelligence workflows. AI-assisted information organization is intended for clinician review. Product concepts are in development; there are no paying customers or live clinical deployments to date.

AdFlow OS: An AI-assisted campaign operations product exploring how teams can move from a brief toward organized creative and campaign workflows.

FOUNDER APPROACH
Build around real problems. Keep technology useful and understandable. Pair long-term ambition with validation, and design healthcare workflows around practitioner oversight.
